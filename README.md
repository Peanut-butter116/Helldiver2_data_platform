# Helldivers 2 China Growth Intelligence Platform (Prototype)

## Assessment Context
This project is a **Streamlit prototype** for a Tencent product/data assessment scenario:
- Game: **Helldivers 2**
- Current Chinese player share: **~8%**
- Strategic objective: grow Chinese player share toward **15%**

## What This Prototype Demonstrates
A clickable, China-first growth intelligence platform that combines:
- First-party gameplay behavior signals
- Tencent-side China community and marketing signals
- Churn attribution and player segmentation outputs
- Channel and social referral analytics
- Experimentation and automation workflows

This is designed as a **product concept demo**, not a production system.

## Pages Included
1. **Overview** – Executive KPI view and growth progress
2. **Behavior Insights** – China-specific behavioral patterns and drop-off insights
3. **Experiments & Automation** – Growth experiments, rule-based interventions, impact trend
4. **Community Signals** – Mock social comment intelligence and viral signal detection
5. **Model Outputs** – Four model modules (churn, clustering, attribution, referral)
6. **Case Study** – “Cyber Shanghai Defence” virality-to-growth framework

## Data Disclaimer
All data in this app is **simulated/mock data for demonstration purposes only**.
No external APIs, databases, or secrets are required.
